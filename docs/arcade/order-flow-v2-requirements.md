# Frozen acceptance requirements — order flow v2

Approved by the user on 28/10/2026. This packet contains observable requirements, not an implementation algorithm. Scope: the existing arcade only, no changes to legacy quiz rules or external deployment.

## Observable behavior

1. Each ordinary public pickup point initially contains one parcel. Taking it starts an independent 45,000ms timer for that point. At the deadline one replacement appears at the same point, regardless of delivery. No second waiting ordinary parcel at a point. Promotional parcels do not occupy or reset ordinary slots. Practice reset at 60,000ms restores the opening wave and clears previous cooldowns.
2. Private channel costs 40 coins once, pays 8/parcel, first parcel at 20,000ms after purchase. Spawn only one parcel per occurrence, at least 20,000ms between occurrences, up to two unclaimed private parcels. A full stock does not accrue a burst of missed spawns. Pausing freezes deadlines.
3. A player can carry up to two of their own private parcels, or one public parcel, never a mix. E takes one parcel per press. E delivers one eligible carried parcel per press, exactly once. If both have the same destination they can be delivered in two presses. Holding E for 1,000ms at own home abandons all carried parcels and pays nothing.
4. Private parcels and carried private parcel identifiers remain unavailable to other players and public spectators. The owner and MC may see authorized private details. Different authenticated players cannot pick up each other's private parcels.
5. Each carried parcel locks its own current P fee at most once on crossing. Carrying two private parcels through P at fee 4 incurs 8 total, settled as 4 net coins per delivered parcel. Existing fee locks survive fee changes; picking a second parcel later does not copy the first parcel's fee lock. Repeated interaction and reconnect must not double-settle.
6. At 180,000ms from MC start, three additional customer destinations become active near the center on the far bank. Existing destinations/orders remain valid. Prior to that milestone new public orders use old destinations. After it, every 20 newly generated public orders (ordinary plus promotion) include 17 central and 3 outer destinations. Central destinations are distributed among the three customers; outer destinations use both left and right over time. Private orders do not affect this ratio. Pickup points remain fixed. Existing orders never change their destination at a phase boundary. Many players can each deliver their separate assigned orders to the same customer.
7. UI: show empty ordinary slot countdowns, distinguish ordinary/promotion/private parcel types, show private waiting stock separately from carried count, support visible two-parcel cargo and accurate per-parcel fee/net proceeds. Central customer arrival is visible at minute 3 and its prior warning describes customers rather than moving pickup locations. Keyboard-only play and existing overlays continue working.
8. Existing room recovery, authority, roles, pause/resume, 60s practice reset, 360s P fee rise, 540s scoring lock and 600s end remain. Old persisted games must be recognized safely; new rules apply to a new run without silently resetting an existing match.
